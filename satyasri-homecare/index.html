About us

Caring for Families Across Secunderabad & Hyderabad

Satyasri Home Care Services was started 8 years ago by M. Sai Durga (Triveni) with one simple aim: to help families in Secunderabad and Hyderabad find dependable care and help at home, whether it's looking after an unwell or elderly parent, caring for little ones, or keeping the house running.

Since then, we have completed more than 2,000 services for families across the twin cities. We work from our own office at Navketan Building, Clock Tower, Secunderabad, so families can always meet us in person, and we stay in touch after a caregiver starts.

Every family is different, so we always begin with a conversation about the person who needs care, their routine, and the hours you need help.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve homes across Secunderabad and Hyderabad. Tell us your area when you call or enquire.

Yes. We provide live-in caretakers who stay round the clock, as well as 12-hour day or night shifts.

Yes. For patient care and elder care you can choose a male or female caregiver. Pick your preference on the enquiry form, or tell us when you call.
